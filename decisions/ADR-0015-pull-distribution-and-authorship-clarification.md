# ADR-0015: Pull distribution and authorship clarification

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-09-04
- Decider: Masahiro Terawaki (CEO)

## Context

ADR-0002 and the business plan identify earned authorship and durable public recognition as the primary outcome. The business plan nevertheless included outbound interviews, invitations, talks, and community participation as default distribution motions. The CEO clarified that the desired outcome is for people to know that Masahiro Terawaki exists; artifacts are instruments for that purpose, not ends in themselves. The CEO also directed that the project use a pull model for now: people who are interested may inquire, but the project should not push outreach.

The public repository has no selected license or reuse policy. Compliance is a non-negotiable boundary; this ADR does not provide legal advice or select a legal instrument.

## Decision

1. Treat credible, attributable discoverability of the author and the author's questions as the current outcome. Treat repository artifacts as evidence and entry points, not as a volume, revenue, or popularity objective.
2. Use pull distribution until the CEO explicitly changes the direction. Keep accurate public materials discoverable, but do not conduct unsolicited outreach, sales, design-partner solicitation, advertising, pitching, or active collaboration recruitment.
3. Consider inbound interest within current authority boundaries. Do not make external commitments, legal/compliance representations, license promises, or real-service commitments without the CEO's explicit approval.
4. Defer the license and third-party reuse decision. Do not infer a legal permission model from the repository's current public visibility or metadata.

## Evidence and assumptions

- **CEO input:** The CEO directly stated the authorship purpose, that artifacts lack independent intrinsic value for the CEO, and that pull distribution is sufficient for now.
- **Repository fact:** The v0.1 business plan describes outbound interviews and invitations that conflict with this direction.
- **Judgment:** A versioned pull-only addendum preserves the older plan's provenance while making the current operating constraint legible.

## Alternatives and trade-offs

- **Continue outbound discovery:** could create faster learning and demand evidence, but conflicts with the CEO's present direction and risks optimizing contact volume rather than meaningful recognition.
- **Make the repository private:** would reduce public exposure but prevents interested third parties from finding the work without a separate discovery channel.
- **Pull distribution:** preserves the stated preference and claim discipline, but may result in slow or no independent discovery.

## Risks and failure conditions

- Absence of outreach may make it impossible to distinguish lack of discovery from lack of interest.
- Optimizing merely for the author's name being seen could undermine the earned-authorship guardrail if work loses evidentiary integrity.
- An unresolved license can create uncertainty for potential readers or contributors; this ADR neither removes that uncertainty nor substitutes for qualified legal advice.

## Reconsider when

- The CEO explicitly authorizes an outbound distribution experiment, with scope and claims bounded in advance.
- Inbound interest creates a concrete request requiring a license, contract, service connection, or legal review.
- Evidence shows that pull distribution prevents the intended audience from finding or understanding the work.
