# ADR-0008: Three-seat covenant and adaptive preservation

**Date:** 2026-09-04  
**Status:** accepted

## Context

The CEO clarifies the intended behavior of the three direct seats. Expansion must continuously open new frontiers. Preservation must integrate learning from Expansion and Critique into the existing system and may propose structural seat changes. Critique must continually seek and report risk rather than merely voice negativity. All three share responsibility for protecting and growing the one purpose.

## Decision

Adopt the Three-Seat Covenant. Establish the operating cycle: Expansion proposes, Critique examines, Preservation integrates, and the strengthened system enables further Expansion. This is a mutual-respect relationship, not an approval hierarchy or permanent conflict.

Preservation owns continuity of purpose rather than inertia of structure. It may propose lower-seat additions, changes, merges, or retirements where required to integrate accepted learning. L1 changes remain CEO decisions.

Critique treats risk as a persistent condition and must report actionable, evidence-bounded findings with alternatives where possible. It does not gain an unconditional veto.
