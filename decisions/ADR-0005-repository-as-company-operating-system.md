# ADR-0005: Repository as a company operating system

**Date:** 2026-09-04  
**Status:** accepted

## Context

The founder intends to use this repository as the operating system for a potentially large company while retaining the role of CEO focused on direction and consequential judgment. The project already treats the repository as an SSOT and Codex as cross-functional execution capacity.

## Decision

Adopt a stable constitutional organization: CEO plus AI Research, Product & Architecture, Operations, Growth, and Risk & Integrity functions. The organization scales through reusable agentic capabilities and evidence loops, not by creating human management layers for routine coordination.

The repository is an operating system and public evidence base; it is **not** by itself a legal company, legal agent, employer, or party to a contract. Legal incorporation, finances, employment, and binding commitments remain separate CEO decisions.

## Trade-offs

The model reduces coordination cost and keeps the founder near the highest-leverage decisions. It also creates risks: automation can create noise, hidden errors can compound, and external relationships require human accountability. The delegation policy therefore makes public, binding, and constitutional actions CEO-controlled by default.

## Reconsider when

A function requires durable external accountability, licensed expertise, or relationship ownership that cannot responsibly be supplied by an AI system and requires a human role or partner.
