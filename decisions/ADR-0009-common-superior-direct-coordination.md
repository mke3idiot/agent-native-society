# ADR-0009: Direct coordination among seats with a common superior

**Date:** 2026-09-04  
**Status:** accepted  
**Amends:** ADR-0007 communication rule

## Context

The CEO corrects the initial communication rule. Mandatory routing through a superior is not required when seats share the same immediate superior. The three CEO-direct seats must be able to meet and coordinate directly.

## Decision

Seats with the same immediate superior may communicate and coordinate directly. This includes the Expansion, Preservation, and Critique three-seat council under the CEO. Direct coordination does not transfer authority reserved to the common superior, nor does it permit one seat to enlarge another's mandate.

Seats with different immediate superiors use their upward reporting path for governance and priority alignment, except for bounded, approved operational interfaces.

## Consequences

- The three executive seats can make the organization faster and more coherent without consuming CEO attention for routine coordination.
- CEO escalation remains mandatory for constitutional, irreversible, or cross-mandate authority changes.
- Council records must preserve the difference between delegated execution, recommendation, and CEO decision.
