# ADR-0006: One purpose, expandable capabilities

**Date:** 2026-09-04  
**Status:** accepted

## Context

The founder clarifies that the organization has one purpose, while roles and end services should expand according to what that purpose requires. A fixed small organization must not block growth; unbounded diversification must not dilute the project.

## Decision

Maintain one governing purpose: advance the transition toward an Agent Native Society. Add roles, AI agents, services, products, and operating loops as subordinate capabilities only when they have a direct, recorded contribution to that purpose.

Organizational permanence resides in the purpose and constitutional decision boundary, not in a fixed list of services or agents.

## Consequences

- A new capability needs an explicit purpose contribution, delegation level, measurable output, and retirement condition.
- Revenue is necessary for sustainable scale but is a constraint and feedback signal, not a separate purpose.
- A highly profitable but unrelated opportunity is rejected unless the CEO deliberately changes the constitution through a new version.
