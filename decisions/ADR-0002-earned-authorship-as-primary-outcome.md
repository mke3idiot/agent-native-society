# ADR-0002: Earned authorship as the primary outcome

**Date:** 2026-09-04  
**Status:** accepted

## Context

The author states that the ultimate aim is to make their name known through this work. The project also rejects unsupported priority claims and values real implementation, truthfulness, and future usefulness.

## Decision

Treat personal recognition as an outcome of **earned authorship**: a visible, attributable, versioned body of original synthesis and demonstrated execution. It is not a license to imply invention, market traction, legal compliance, or technical capability that has not been evidenced.

Business activity is a validation and distribution mechanism, not an obligation to form a company prematurely. Each commercial step must either create evidence, create real user value, fund further work, or expand the author's credible network.

## Alternatives and trade-offs

- **Attention-first branding:** can grow reach quickly but creates a high risk of shallow claims, standard-chasing, and credibility loss.
- **Stealth startup first:** may protect focus but delays public provenance and the authorship objective.
- **Pure research archive:** protects depth but may not create enough real-world validation or distribution.

The selected path is a public research-to-implementation venture studio model: publish credible artifacts, use them to convene conversations and paid/funded validation work, and turn repeated implementation lessons into reusable infrastructure.

## Risks and failure conditions

The project fails this decision if publishing outpaces evidence, if the author becomes identified only with a transient protocol, or if consulting activity consumes all capacity without producing reusable public learning.

## Reconsider when

Recognition creates pressure to make claims inconsistent with the evidence, or a different operating model demonstrably produces stronger public provenance and real-world learning.
