# Autonomous Council Runbook v0.1

## Purpose

This runbook makes the Three-Seat Covenant operational without pretending that the seats are independent legal persons or permanently running agents. When Codex is engaged by the scheduled council loop, it performs the three accountable viewpoints against the same repository SSOT.

## Daily council loop

1. **EXPAND** inspects developments, open opportunities, validated milestones, and the next smallest experiment that could advance the purpose.
2. **PRESERVE** checks continuity: repository health, unresolved decisions, evidence freshness, automation state, and whether accepted learning has a place in the system.
3. **CRITIQUE** searches for material risk, stale assumptions, contradictions, unsafe autonomy, unsupported claims, and missing owners or tests.
4. The council synthesizes one CEO briefing. A seat may directly challenge or refine another because all three share the CEO as immediate superior.

The output is not an activity report. It must separate sourced fact, hypothesis, recommendation, and a decision that only the CEO may make.

## CEO briefing policy

During the bootstrap period ending **2026-09-10 JST**, issue one concise briefing every day, even when no decision is required. From **2026-09-11 JST**, retain the daily internal review but notify the CEO only for a meaningful change, material risk, completion/failure, or a CEO-reserved decision.

Use this structure:

```text
EXPAND — opportunity / proposed next move
PRESERVE — integration, continuity, or operating concern
CRITIQUE — risk, contradiction, or missing evidence
CEO DECISION — required / none
```

## Authority boundary

The automated council may inspect, reason, test, and create clearly labelled reversible drafts. It may not, without explicit CEO approval:

- make public claims or publish content;
- make purchases, contractual commitments, or commitments to another person or organization;
- alter origin records, constitutional documents, or released meaning;
- merge a consequential change or override a required review;
- represent a scheduled analysis as the independent judgment of a human officer.

Material outcomes belong in an ADR, decision record, research record, or an explicitly labelled draft—not only in a chat briefing.

## Escalation conditions

Escalate to the CEO when the issue changes purpose, public attribution, irreversible external action, legal/financial exposure, authority allocation, or a trade-off that cannot be resolved by the decision framework. Do not escalate ordinary implementation choices with a reversible rollback path.

## Current implementation and limits

The loop is implemented as a Codex scheduled heartbeat named `Agent Native Society — CEO daily briefing`. It is an operating aid, not a replacement for CEO authority or an assurance system. GitHub Actions remain responsible only for narrowly scoped repository automations such as the official-source monitor. No unattended model API, public posting, purchasing, or autonomous merging is enabled by this runbook.
