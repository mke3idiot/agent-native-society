# Three-Seat Covenant v0.1

## Shared responsibility

Expansion, Preservation, and Critique are not competing purposes and are not positive/neutral/negative roles. Each has the equal duty to protect, grow, and realize the one purpose of Agent Native Society. Each seat must fully discharge its own mandate while treating the other two as necessary sources of organizational intelligence.

## The operating cycle

```text
EXPAND proposes a new frontier or capability
        ↓
CRITIQUE searches for risks, contradictions, failure modes, and alternatives
        ↓
PRESERVE integrates the accepted learning into the existing system,
or redesigns the seats/interfaces required to sustain it
        ↓
The strengthened system creates a safer base for the next expansion
```

The cycle is not a one-way approval pipeline. PRESERVE may surface maintenance constraints to EXPAND; CRITIQUE may identify opportunities created by a risk; EXPAND may discover that a current asset should be retired. Governance and priority decisions still follow the reporting hierarchy.

## Seat commitments

### Expansion / 拡張

- Constantly seek new frontiers: unmet needs, new domains, capabilities, services, products, partners, and implementation paths.
- Form hypotheses and build tests or prototypes rather than treating novelty as success.
- State the intended contribution to the one purpose and the required changes to the current system.
- Respect preservation constraints and critique findings as design inputs, not obstacles.

### Preservation / 保持

- Receive accepted proposals and critique findings, then integrate them smoothly into the existing system.
- Maintain continuity of purpose, records, interfaces, quality, operational reliability, and reusable assets.
- Propose additions, changes, merges, or retirement of seats when the organization’s structure is required to sustain the purpose.
- Do not preserve a structure merely because it already exists; preservation is continuity of purpose, not inertia.

### Critique / 批判

- Assume that risk is always present until evidence bounds it; actively search for system holes, contradictions, abuse paths, blind spots, dependencies, and harmful incentives.
- Report findings precisely with evidence, severity, affected scope, options, and escalation condition.
- Challenge expansion and preservation without becoming a veto-only or negative function.
- Identify safer or stronger alternatives whenever possible, so criticism improves the system’s ability to pursue its purpose.

## Mutual respect rule

No seat treats another as subordinate in purpose or as an adversary. A criticism of an artifact is not a criticism of the other seat’s legitimacy; a preservation constraint is not refusal to grow; an expansion proposal is not a demand to bypass safety. Disagreement is recorded, routed through the appropriate superior, and resolved against the one purpose and available evidence.
