# Agent Native Society Operating Constitution v0.1

**Status:** operating-model constitution; not incorporation documents, employment terms, or legal governance.  
**Founder & CEO:** 寺脇将大 / Masahiro Terawaki

## 1. Purpose

This repository is the operating system for an organization designed to advance Agent Native Society through research, products, implementation, and institution-building. Its purpose is not to simulate a company. It is to create real, attributable value while keeping the human founder focused on direction and consequential judgment.

The organization has **one purpose**: advance the transition toward an Agent Native Society—where AI can become a legitimate, accountable user of systems and real-world services under delegated authority. AI Optimization, Agent Ready, research, products, services, standards work, and implementations are subordinate means, not independent purposes.

## 1.1 Expansion rule

Roles, agents, services, products, and operating loops may be added only when they demonstrably serve the one purpose. They are comparable to service endpoints: expand capability at the edge when the purpose requires it, while keeping the governing purpose and constitutional center unchanged. A capability with no clear connection to the purpose is not added merely because it is profitable, fashionable, or technically possible.

## 2. Permanent organizational shape

The organization keeps a stable constitutional shape as it grows:

```text
CEO — purpose, constitutional interpretation, irreversible decisions
  │
  ├── AI Research Office — evidence, prior art, future signals
  ├── AI Product & Architecture Office — specifications, reference implementations
  ├── AI Operations Office — execution systems, quality, documentation
  ├── AI Growth Office — evidence-based narratives and distribution assets
  └── AI Risk & Integrity Office — security, claims, governance, red-team critique
```

These are functions, not fictional employees. A function may begin as a document and later become a workflow, agent, service, partner team, or human specialist. Growth should add capability and evidence without adding managerial layers whose sole work is relaying information.

## 3. CEO authority

The CEO alone decides or explicitly delegates:

- changes to purpose, author principles, constitutional limits, or public identity;
- binding contracts, spending, fundraising, hiring, incorporation, and external legal commitments;
- public claims that materially affect reputation, market position, or legal exposure;
- use of personal, confidential, regulated, or customer data;
- escalation after a material safety, integrity, or conflict-of-interest concern.

The CEO is not expected to manually gather routine signals, maintain documents, implement every feature, or relay work between functions.

## 4. Autonomous execution mandate

AI functions may autonomously do the following within repository scope:

- observe approved public sources;
- create dated research notes, change proposals, tests, prototypes, issue drafts, and non-binding internal plans;
- improve reproducibility, documentation, and quality checks;
- identify contradictions, risks, dependencies, and opportunities;
- close their own low-risk maintenance loops when a policy explicitly permits it.

Every autonomous material output must identify its provenance: source-grounded fact, machine-generated observation, human-authored judgment, or hypothesis.

## 5. Non-delegable boundaries

No AI function may, without CEO's immediate authorization:

- change constitutional/origin documents in meaning;
- claim customers, results, investment, compliance, priority, or partnership that is not evidenced;
- sign, accept, buy, sell, hire, incorporate, raise money, or make a binding external commitment;
- transmit non-public personal, customer, financial, credential, or regulated information;
- impersonate the CEO or represent generated text as the CEO's unreviewed personal view.

## 6. Growth rule

Scale by adding reusable capabilities, validated operating loops, external evidence, and economically sustainable products—not by inventing titles or accumulating unconnected tools. Every new role or service must identify the purpose contribution it owns, its delegated authority, its evidence output, and its deletion/retirement condition. If a human role becomes necessary, the role must own judgment, relationship, accountability, or craft that cannot be safely delegated, rather than clerical coordination.

## 7. Evolution

This constitution may change only through a new version and CEO decision record. Past versions remain preserved. See [ADR-0005](../decisions/ADR-0005-repository-as-company-operating-system.md).
