## Reading of the relationship

MCP is useful for tool and context access; A2A concerns agent interoperability; UCP applies interoperable capability patterns to commerce. None alone resolves the complete societal question of legitimate delegated agency across services. Conversely, this repository is not a replacement specification for any of them. It is a framework for evaluating and designing services that may use them.
