## Interpretation (author/Codex hypothesis)

The likely durable gap is not another transport specification. It is operationalizing agent-mediated action at the commitment boundary: portable semantics, bounded delegation, policy enforcement, evidence, and recourse. The business opportunity is therefore an **Agent Readiness practice and infrastructure**, beginning with a narrow vertical and treating protocols as adapters.
